Relevancy points

The three densest spots on the heatmap or grid. Observed calls within the radius against the number expected if the home community area's calls were spread evenly.

PointObs.Exp.LQAdj. pRelevant

"Relevant" means adj. p < 0.05 and at least 5 calls. Calls cluster more than the Poisson test assumes, so it is a screen, not proof. Source: City of Chicago 311 (v6vf-nfxy), 2026.
